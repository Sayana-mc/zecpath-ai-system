\# AI Ethics Documentation



\## 1. Purpose



The purpose of this review is to ensure that the HR AI system follows responsible AI principles during candidate screening and interview evaluation.



\## 2. Candidate Consent



Candidate consent is required before AI-based processing.



The system records whether consent has been provided.



If consent is not available, AI processing should be blocked.



\## 3. Human Oversight



AI-generated scores and recommendations should support human decision-making rather than completely replace human judgment.



Human review should remain available for uncertain or low-confidence cases.



\## 4. Fairness



The scoring system should evaluate job-relevant factors such as:



\- Skills

\- Experience

\- Communication

\- Consistency

\- Role relevance



Demographic characteristics should not be used as scoring factors.



\## 5. Demographic Bias Signals



The review checks for demographic signals such as:



\- Gender

\- Age

\- Religion

\- Race

\- Ethnicity

\- Caste

\- Disability

\- Nationality

\- Marital status



These signals should not influence candidate scoring.



\## 6. Explainability



The system provides explanations based on score components.



Examples include:



\- Relevant skills contributed to the score.

\- Relevant experience contributed to the score.

\- Communication performance contributed to the score.

\- Response consistency contributed to the score.



\## 7. Data Protection



Candidate information should only be retained for an approved business purpose and defined retention period.



\## 8. Human Review



Candidates should have a path for human review when automated results are uncertain.



\## 9. Limitations



This document represents an AI ethics readiness review.



It does not certify legal or regulatory compliance.



Legal and privacy requirements should be reviewed by the organization's appropriate legal/compliance team.
