\# Day 43 – Ethics \& Compliance Review



\## Objective



Ensure that the HR AI system follows responsible AI principles and is prepared for further compliance review.



\## Tasks Completed



\- Documented candidate consent requirements

\- Reviewed fairness of scoring

\- Detected demographic bias signals

\- Removed demographic signals from analysis text

\- Added explainability notes

\- Added human-review controls

\- Added configurable data-retention logic

\- Generated ethics and compliance reports



\## Main Components



\- Consent Manager

\- Bias Signal Detector

\- Fairness Reviewer

\- Explainability Generator

\- Retention Manager

\- Ethics \& Compliance Engine



\## Deliverables



1\. AI Ethics Documentation

2\. Fairness Review Notes

3\. Compliance Readiness Report



\## Outputs



\- ethics\_review.json

\- fairness\_review.json

\- compliance\_readiness\_report.json
