\# Day 42 – Optimization \& Stability



\## Objective



Improve the reliability, consistency, and processing efficiency

of the HR Interview AI system.



\## Key Optimization Areas



1\. False positive / false negative reduction

2\. Follow-up logic stability

3\. Scoring anomaly detection

4\. Processing speed improvement

5\. Transcript cleanup



\## Main Deliverables



\### 1. Stable HR Interview AI



Provides controlled follow-up behavior, cleaned transcripts,

and validated scoring.



\### 2. Optimization Report



Documents optimization techniques, testing, performance,

and limitations.



\### 3. Refined Scoring Engine



Validates scores, handles missing values, and detects

potential scoring anomalies.
