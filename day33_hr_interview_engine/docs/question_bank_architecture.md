\# Question Bank Architecture



\## Categories



\### 1. Self Introduction

Used to understand the candidate's background and introduction.



\### 2. Career Journey

Used to understand education, career motivation and previous journey.



\### 3. Strengths \& Weaknesses

Used to understand self-awareness and development areas.



\### 4. Teamwork \& Culture Fit

Used to understand collaboration and workplace interaction.



\### 5. Career Goals

Used to understand future career direction.



\### 6. Availability \& Commitment

Used to understand joining availability and commitment.



\## Question Metadata



Each question contains:



\- Question ID

\- Question text

\- Category

\- Role type

\- Experience level



\## Role-Based Generation



Experience levels:



\- Fresher

\- Experienced



Role types:



\- Technical

\- Non-Technical



The question generator filters questions according to

experience level and role type.
