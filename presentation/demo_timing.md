## 2. Presenter Pacing Markers & Transition Cues

* **Minute 05:00 Cue:** Transition from slides to browser. "Now that we've seen the architectural blueprint, let's step into the shoes of a recruiter using the live system."
* **Minute 10:00 Cue:** Completing ATS inspection. "ATS scoring gets candidates to the starting line, but human technical ability can only be evaluated through dynamic conversation. Let's look at the conversational screening engine."
* **Minute 15:30 Cue:** Transition to unified aggregation. "Notice how isolated scores from five different rounds are synthesized into one explainable fit percentage."
* **Minute 18:30 Cue:** Wrap up live demo. "Every action we just performed was captured in real-time by the observability engine. Let's conclude with the business impact."

## 3. Buffer Contingency Strategies

* **If running 2 minutes ahead:** Deepen the comparison between candidate `CAND-006` (career switcher) and `CAND-008` (legacy developer) to emphasize explainability and fairness.
* **If running 2 minutes behind:** Shorten the slide review in Stage 02 and proceed directly to the live demo; summarize the technical interview rubrics verbally while viewing the unified fit dossier.
