\# Day 40 – Improvement Recommendations



\## 1. Improve Communication Scoring



Communication scoring can be improved by considering:



\- Sentence structure

\- Grammar

\- Vocabulary

\- Response organization

\- Filler words



\## 2. Improve Confidence Detection



Confidence analysis can use:



\- Hesitation phrases

\- Repeated words

\- Uncertainty expressions

\- Response completeness

\- Consistency across answers



\## 3. Improve Manual vs AI Agreement



The scoring model should be periodically compared with

human evaluation.



Large score differences should be reviewed and used to

adjust scoring rules.



\## 4. Reduce False Rejections



Candidates should not be rejected only because of:



\- Short answers

\- Limited experience

\- Minor grammar mistakes

\- Hesitation



Additional contextual signals should be considered.



\## 5. Improve Candidate-Type Handling



The system should distinguish between:



\- Confident candidates

\- Hesitant candidates

\- Freshers

\- Experienced candidates

\- Overqualified candidates



\## 6. Future Improvements



Future versions can include:



\- Speech-to-text integration

\- Real-time conversation analysis

\- LLM-based semantic evaluation

\- Advanced sentiment analysis

\- Adaptive follow-up questions

\- Historical interview comparison
