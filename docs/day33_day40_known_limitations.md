# Days 33–40: Known Limitations & Safeguards

## 1. Architectural Limitations
1. **Textual Proxy for Audio Nuances:**
   - Communication evaluation evaluates transcribed lexical and syntactic patterns. Subtle vocal cadence, pitch variations, and acoustic inflection are not directly modeled in textual mode.
2. **Deterministic Heuristic Rubrics:**
   - Heuristic matching handles standard behavioral keywords and connectors. Extremely abstract or highly metaphorical answers may receive conservative relevance scores and require human recruiter review.
3. **Single Adaptive Follow-Up Limit:**
   - To prevent interview fatigue and keep scheduling predictable, each question is limited to one adaptive follow-up.

---

## 2. Ethical & Regulatory Safeguards
1. **No Psychological Profiling:**
   - The system strictly measures communication clarity, hesitation markers, and consistency. It does NOT diagnose or infer personality traits, mental health, emotional state, or psychological stability.
2. **Regional Accent Neutrality:**
   - The evaluation does not penalize dialect, non-standard syntactic idioms, or phoneme variations.
3. **Mandatory Human-in-the-Loop:**
   - AI evaluations and suggested recommendations (`PROCEED`, `HOLD`, `REJECT`) are advisory only. Final employment decisions remain under the exclusive control of authorized human recruiters.
