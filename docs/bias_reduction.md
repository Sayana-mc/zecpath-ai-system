# ZecPath Day 15 – Fairness, Normalization & Bias Reduction

## 1. Objective

Day 15 improves the fairness and consistency of the ZecPath resume
evaluation pipeline.

The main objectives are:

- Normalize candidate records.
- Standardize score ranges.
- Reduce excessive dependence on explicit keywords.
- Mask non-essential personal attributes.
- Prevent protected/personal attributes from influencing candidate scores.
- Calculate bias-risk indicators.
- Produce transparent fairness reports for recruiter review.

The Day 15 module is designed to process the ranked candidate records
generated by the previous ATS/ranking stage.
