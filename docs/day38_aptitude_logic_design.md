# Day 38: Reasoning & Situational Judgment Aptitude Logic

## 1. Overview
The Aptitude Engine evaluates candidate cognitive flexibility, logical deduction, situational judgment, and structured decision-making without requiring complex algorithmic coding rounds.

---

## 2. Aptitude Categories

1. **Logical Reasoning (`logical_reasoning`):**
   - Arithmetic deduction, proportion calculation, and step-by-step problem deconstruction.
2. **Problem Solving (`problem_solving`):**
   - Resolving production incidents, emergency post-mortems, and systematic root cause discovery.
3. **Situational Judgment (`situational_judgment`):**
   - Prioritizing conflicting tasks with identical deadlines across internal engineering and external clients.
4. **Decision Making (`decision_making`):**
   - Acting decisively amidst incomplete requirements or ambiguous business guidelines.

---

## 3. Multi-Dimensional Rubric Scoring
Every aptitude answer is scored across four orthogonal dimensions:
- **Logic Score (30%):** Step-by-step deduction and causal connectors (`because`, `therefore`, `first`, `then`).
- **Problem Solving Score (25%):** Application of structural frameworks (trade-off matrices, mitigation plans).
- **Decision Making Score (25%):** Decisiveness, prioritization under constraints, stakeholder transparency.
- **Communication Score (20%):** Articulation and conciseness.
