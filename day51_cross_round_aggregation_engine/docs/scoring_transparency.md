

\# Scoring Transparency and Safeguards



\## What the candidate score should explain

\- Which evaluation stages were completed.

\- Each raw score and maximum possible score.

\- The normalized score for each round.

\- Configured and effective round weights.

\- Each round's weighted contribution.

\- The formula used to calculate the final score.

\- Missing or not-applicable evaluation stages.

\- Scoring configuration version.



\## Fairness and governance

\- Use job-related, consistently applied evaluation criteria.

\- Review the validity of each upstream score.

\- Provide reasonable accommodations and equivalent conditions.

\- Avoid using protected characteristics or unrelated personal details.

\- Audit score differences and assess potential bias.

\- Record human review and allow correction of inaccurate evaluation data.

\- Do not use this prototype as the sole basis for hiring or rejection.



\## Missing-round policy

Missing and pending stages are not automatically scored as zero.

Their weights are excluded and the remaining weights are renormalized.

The output is marked PARTIAL until required rounds are completed.



\## Prototype limitation

The engine calculates scores from supplied inputs; it does not prove that those

inputs are fair, accurate, or valid. Human review is required.
